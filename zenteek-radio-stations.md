# Zenteek — Radio Stations

A curated list of internet radio streams for [Zenteek](https://zenteek.app/), grouped by sound and quality. Paste a stream URL into Zenteek to add it.

> Formats: `.m3u8` = HLS · `.pls` = playlist (resolves to the stream) · `.flac`/`-flacm` = lossless · `.ogg`/`.mp3` = lossy.
> Many hi-res picks are tracked at [audiophileradio.com](https://audiophileradio.com/) (launched Dec 2025, streams checked daily — 117 stations as of this update). **Radio Paradise** also has a newer **KFAT** (folk) channel listed there; no direct stream URL found yet.
