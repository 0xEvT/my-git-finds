# Zenteek — Radio Stations

A curated list of internet radio streams for [Zenteek](https://zenteek.app/), grouped by sound and quality. Paste a stream URL into Zenteek to add it. Lossless (FLAC) streams are listed first.

> Formats: `.m3u8` = HLS · `.pls` = playlist (resolves to the stream) · `.flac`/`-flacm` = lossless · `.ogg`/`.mp3` = lossy.
